01 The boundary rule

A vertex on an edge may use only the labels of the edge’s two endpoints. Each triangle’s original corners are forced to have labels 0, 1, 2. Interior vertices can take any of the three labels.

02 Why the count is odd

Sperner’s lemma guarantees an odd number of triangles carrying all three labels. Refining the mesh or changing the valid coloring changes the count, but never its parity.
